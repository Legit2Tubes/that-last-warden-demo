Download the Windows ZIP from the release's **Assets** section, extract it, and launch `TheLastWarden.exe`.

### Controls

- **WASD / Arrow Keys:** Face incoming enemies
- **Spacebar:** Use equipped power-up

### How to Play

Survive waves of enemies approaching from four directions.

- **Goblin:** Face it to defeat it.
- **Skeleton:** Face away from it.
- **Mage:** Watch for its opposite-lane teleport.
- **Courier:** Collect power-ups or gold.

One unprotected hit ends your run. Survive as long as possible and beat your high score!
