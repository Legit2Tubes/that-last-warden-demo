# The Last Warden — Windows Playable Demo v0.1.0

The first Windows demo brings the watch to a torchlit four-lane courtyard.
Turn to survive Goblins, teleporting Mages and watchful Skeletons, collect
Couriers, and choose the moment to use your one held item.

- Endless encounters with increasing speed, mixed patterns and brief recovery beats.
- Shield, Clock and Nova, with animated inventory feedback and visible protection.
- Kill scoring, capped combos, persistent gold and personal bests.
- Directional character poses, enemy defeat effects and distinct defeat sounds.
- Three-track soundtrack rotation and separate music, effects and defeat volumes.
- Native menus, pause/retry, resizable windows and F11/Alt+Enter fullscreen.

**Download:** `TheLastWarden-Windows-v0.1.0.zip` (Windows x86_64).
Extract everything and run `TheLastWarden.exe`; keep its `.pck` beside it.
The `SHA256SUMS.txt` release attachment records the archive checksum.

This is a prepared local release candidate. Public distribution is pending asset
license confirmation and owner approval. Physical controller/touch hardware,
older Windows versions and audible balance remain unverified. The executable is
unsigned. See [README](README.md) for controls, requirements and bug reports,
and [credits](CREDITS.md) for the outstanding license records.
